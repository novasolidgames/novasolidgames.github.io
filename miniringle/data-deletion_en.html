App and Developer

App: MiniRingle: 4 Player Offline (Google Play, package com.novasolidgames.miniringle)

Developer: 노바솔리드게임즈 (NovaSolidGames)

The developer does not hold any of your game data. The app has no sign-up, accounts or developer servers, and game data is stored only on your device and, if you signed in, in Google Play Games. You can delete your data yourself as described below.

1. Game Data on Your Device

How to delete: uninstall the app, or open your device Settings > Apps > MiniRingle > Storage > Clear data.

Deleted: game progress (Gold, unlocked games, favorites, cosmetic items, player colors, best scores, number of plays, rewards claimed), settings, the age you selected, your consent record and the confirmation record for Remove Ads.

Kept: the Remove Ads purchase itself stays in your Google Play purchase history, so you can restore it after reinstalling. Google Play Games data is deleted separately as described in section 2.

2. Google Play Games Data (Leaderboards, Achievements, Cloud Save)

This data exists only if you played while signed in to Google Play Games on Android. It is stored by Google, and the developer does not receive it.

How to delete: in the Google Play Games app (or the Play Games profile in the Google Play Store app), go to Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data > Delete individual game data, choose MiniRingle and delete it. On the web you can manage it at play.google.com/games/profile. Menu names may differ slightly by app version.

Deleted: this game’s leaderboard entries, achievements and their progress, and cloud save.

Kept: your Play Games profile itself and data for other games. To delete your whole Play Games profile, use the account deletion option in the same menu or manage it in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).

3. Advertising Data

Information processed by the advertising providers (LevelPlay by Unity Technologies / ironSource, and Google AdMob) is kept under their own policies. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads to opt out of personalized ads linked to it.
