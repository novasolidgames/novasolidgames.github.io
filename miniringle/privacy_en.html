The Service works without servers or accounts, and the Company does not receive or keep your game data on its servers. If this policy changes, we will notify you in the game.

2. Information We Process

  1. Information kept only on your device: game progress, Gold, cosmetics, settings, the age you selected, your consent record, and the order confirmation for the Remove Ads purchase. This information is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Company.

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, password or precise device location.

3. Purposes of Use

We use the information to save game progress, provide ads and ad rewards, apply child-directed ads, confirm and restore purchases, process refunds and answer support requests.

4. Retention and Deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app’s data in your device settings. The Company keeps no copy, so it cannot be recovered after deletion. Support emails are deleted once handled, except records that laws require us to keep (such as records of consumer complaints or disputes, for 3 years).
