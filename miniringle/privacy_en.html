MiniRingle: 4 Player Offline

Privacy Policy

Summary

MiniRingle: 4 Player Offline (the “Service”) has no sign-up, and 노바솔리드게임즈 (NovaSolidGames, the “Company”) runs no server and receives no personal information from you. Your game progress is stored on your device, and on Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, leaderboards, achievements and a copy of your game progress (cloud save) are stored by Google. The Service shows ads and sells “Remove Ads” through Google Play billing. Information needed for ads, payments and Google Play Games goes directly from your device to Google and the other providers below and is handled under their policies.

The Company maintains this policy under Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act to protect your personal information and rights.

1. Information Processed and How It Is Collected

  1. Collected directly by the Company: none. The Company does not receive your name, contact details, account, payment details or location. Only if you email us do we receive your email address, the content of your message and any order number you provide.

  2. Stored on your device: game progress (Gold, unlocked games, favorites, cosmetic items and what is equipped, player colors, best score per game, number of plays, rewards claimed), settings (music and sound volume, language, display settings), the age you selected, your consent record, and the order confirmation record for Remove Ads. This information is not sent to the Company and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

  3. Google Play Games (optional, Android): if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends the following to Google through Google Play Games Services. The Company does not receive this information; Google stores and processes it linked to your Play Games profile.

    · Leaderboards: your best score per game. Your Play Games nickname and profile picture may be visible to other players on leaderboards, as set in your Play Games profile settings.

    · Achievements: achievements unlocked and their progress.

    · Cloud save: a copy of the game progress and settings listed above (the copy also contains the age and Remove Ads record, but when loaded on another device that device’s own values are used). It is used to recover your progress on another device or after reinstalling. If the progress on your device and in the cloud differ, the game asks you which one to use.

  4. In-app purchase (optional): payment for Remove Ads is handled by the Google Play billing system, and the Company does not receive payment method details. To apply and restore the purchase, the game checks the store’s purchase record (product ID, order number and purchase state) on your device.

  5. Advertising: the SDKs of the advertising providers (LevelPlay by Unity Technologies / ironSource, and Google AdMob) may process the advertising ID (Android advertising ID), device model, operating system, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information.

  6. Age signals: age information from Google Play (Play Age Signals) is checked on your device only to decide whether to switch to child-directed ads. It is not stored or sent to the Company.

2. Purposes of Use

  1. To save your game progress on your device so you can continue playing and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, to let you recover it on another device or after reinstalling through cloud save.

  2. To provide Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements.

  3. To apply and restore a purchased Remove Ads and handle refund requests.

  4. To show ads, give ad rewards and apply age-appropriate (child-directed) ads. Frequency capping, fraud prevention and performance measurement are carried out by the advertising providers.

  5. To answer your inquiries.

3. Retention

Information on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google Play Games leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves stay with Google until you delete this game’s data in Play Games or delete your Play Games profile. Support emails are deleted once handled, except records that laws require us to keep (such as records of consumer complaints or disputes, for 3 years). Information processed by advertising and payment providers is kept under their own policies.

4. Destruction

Because the Company receives no personal information through the Service, it holds no Service data to destroy. Information on your device is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data, and the Company cannot recover it because it keeps no copy (if you have a Google Play Games cloud save, you can load it yourself). Clearing app data does not delete Google Play Games data; delete it as described in Section 8. Support emails past their retention period are deleted so they cannot be viewed again.

5. Provision to Third Parties

Because the Company receives no personal information, it does not provide or sell it to third parties. Information needed for ads, payments and Google Play Games goes directly from your device to the providers below.

7. Children

The Service has no age limit. If you select an age under 14 or Google Play reports that you are a minor, we ask the advertising providers to treat ad requests as child-directed so that only non-personalized ads are shown. In that case the game does not show the Google Play Games sign-in screen and uses leaderboards, achievements and cloud save only if a Play Games profile is already signed in on the device (a Google Account under 13 needs a parent’s approval to create a Play Games profile). The Company does not receive children’s personal information.

8. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  1. You can delete the game data on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.

  2. You can delete this game’s Google Play Games data (leaderboard entries, achievements and cloud save) by deleting the game’s data in the Google Play Games app settings or deleting your Play Games profile. You can change what others see on leaderboards in your Play Games profile settings. If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, this data is not created.

  3. You can opt out of personalized ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Ads may still be shown.

  4. For payments and refunds, you can contact Google Play under its refund policy or email the Company under the Terms of Service.

  5. For any other request to access, correct or delete personal information, stop processing or withdraw consent, email us at the address below. We will reply within 10 days.

9. Security
