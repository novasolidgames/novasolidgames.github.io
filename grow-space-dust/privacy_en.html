Privacy Policy

1. Overview

NovaSolidGames (the “Company”) processes personal data as described below to provide the game Grow Space Dust: Idle Clicker (the “Service”). If this policy changes, the Company will announce it in the game.

2. Data We Collect

  1. Account: guest account identifier, Player ID, records of terms consent and withdrawal requests, and, if a Google account is linked, the Google account identifier and email address

  2. Game use: nickname, game progress the user saves to the server, ranking score and country, access records

  3. Device: device model, OS and app version, language, IP address and the country estimated from it

  4. Advertising: advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), device information, ad viewing records (collected by the ad partners’ SDKs)

  5. Purchases: order number, purchase verification data, product, amount and time, refund records (no card numbers or other payment method details)

  6. Support: email address, Player ID, inquiry content, device information

Google Play age information is used only to check eligibility and is not stored. The Company does not collect names, phone numbers, addresses, passwords or precise device location.

3. Purposes

Account management and eligibility checks, saving and restoring game data, rankings, ads and ad rewards, purchase verification and refunds, fraud prevention, customer support and service improvement.

4. Retention and Deletion

Data is deleted once its purpose is fulfilled. Withdrawal is handled under the Terms of Service, and records that the law requires to be kept are kept for that period. Data stored on the device can be cleared with the in-game data clearing option or by uninstalling the app.

6. Users’ Rights

Users may request access to, correction or deletion of their personal data, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent through ‘Lobby > Settings > Delete Account’, ‘Lobby > Settings > Contact Us’, or the email below.

7. Age

The Service is for users aged 14 or older. If the Company learns that data of a child under 14 has been collected, it deletes it. Where the law of the user’s country of residence sets its own minimum age or parental consent requirement, that law applies.

The Company takes reasonable measures such as encrypting communications and restricting access to admin tools.
