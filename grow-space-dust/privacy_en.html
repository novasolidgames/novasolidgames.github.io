Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Korean Privacy Policy. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails to the extent permitted by the laws of the country where you live.

1. About This Privacy Policy

노바솔리드게임즈 (NovaSolidGames, the “Company”) processes users’ personal data as described below in order to provide Grow Space Dust: Idle Clicker (the “Service”).

The Company values users’ personal data and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains for what purposes and in what ways users’ personal data is used and how it is protected.

This Policy can be viewed at any time on the consent screen shown when the game is first started and in ‘Lobby > Settings > View Terms’.

When the Company amends this Policy, it announces the change from 7 days before the effective date through a notice shown when the game starts and on the store’s app page. If important matters such as the data collected, the purposes of use or provision to third parties change, the Company announces it from 30 days before, asks for consent again when the game starts, and does not treat a user as having agreed to the changes merely because the user did not object.

2. Personal Data We Collect

The Company collects only the minimum data needed to provide the Service. The Company does not collect users’ names, phone numbers, addresses, passwords or resident registration numbers. Users may refuse to consent to the collection and use of personal data, but in that case they cannot use the Service.

When collected

Data collected

Guest account identifier created on the device, Player ID assigned by the Service, consent records (type, version and language of the documents agreed to, an identifier of the document text shown on screen, time of consent, app version), and confirmation of being 14 or older. Advertising identifier (Android advertising ID), device information, IP address, and ad viewing and interaction records. Check-in type, round, day number, kind and amount of the reward, a request identifier, and the approval time. Check-in dates and other free rewards received are recorded only on the device and are not sent to the server.

When receiving a reward by mail or coupon

Identifier of the mail or coupon, the reward granted, and the approval time

When contacting customer support

Email address, Player ID and the content of the inquiry. When contacting us in the game, the app version, operating system and device model are sent together, and if the user has purchased paid products, the unspent amount of purchased currency, the numbers of purchases and refunds, and recent order numbers and their processing status are also sent.

The Company collects personal data in the following ways.

  1. When the user first starts the game, agrees to the Terms of Service and to the collection and use of personal data, and confirms being 14 or older, an account is created and data is collected at that time. Before the user agrees, no account is created and no data is sent to the server or to external companies’ programs (SDKs).

  2. When the user enters or performs something directly, such as a nickname, Google account linking or Server Save.

  3. Device information, access logs and the like are generated and collected automatically in the course of using the Service.

  4. Data may be collected through email and the like in the course of customer inquiries.

3. How We Use Personal Data

The Company uses personal data only for the following purposes.

  1. Member management, such as creating and identifying accounts, age confirmation and confirming account deletion requests

  2. Keeping and loading game progress on the server, and recovering the account when the device changes

  3. Providing features in which the nickname, score and country (flag) are visible to other users, such as rankings

  4. Providing ads and granting rewards for watching ads

  5. Confirming and delivering purchases of paid products, recovering purchase history, confirming refunds and recovering refunded items, and calculating partial refunds of the Check-in Pass

  6. Preventing duplicate grants of check-in rewards and of mail and coupon rewards

  7. Preventing and sanctioning abuse, operating naming policies such as for nicknames, keeping records for dispute resolution, and handling customer inquiries

  8. Producing usage statistics and improving the Service. This may be done in a form that does not identify specific individuals.

  9. Delivering notices such as changes to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, and recording and proving consent

Where the laws of the country where the user lives require the legal basis for processing to be stated, the Company processes accounts, game progress, rankings and purchases to perform the service agreement; keeps consent records and transaction records to comply with legal obligations; prevents abuse and produces usage statistics for its legitimate interest in operating the Service safely; and uses advertising identifiers on the basis of the user’s consent or another basis permitted by applicable laws.

5. Retention and Destruction of Personal Data

  1. Users’ personal data is destroyed without delay upon account deletion or when the purpose of collection has been achieved.

  2. When the user requests account deletion, use of the account is restricted immediately, and to allow the request to be canceled, account information, the nickname, game progress kept on the server and related records are kept for 30 days from the request and then deleted without undue delay. During that period they are processed only as necessary for cancellation and resumption, identity verification, refunds and legal obligations. If the user cancels within 30 days, use resumes with the retained information. If the user requests immediate deletion, the data is deleted without undue delay after review. Requests made in the game and by email (section 6) are handled the same way.

  3. Deleting data on the server does not remotely erase files on the device. Game progress, sign-in information and consent records stored on the device can be cleared with [Clear data on this device] in the game, by clearing the app data in the device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

  4. Consent records are kept until the account is deleted.

  5. Nicknames, scores and countries already registered in rankings may remain visible until that ranking period (daily, weekly or monthly) resets, which is at most 1 month.

  6. Deleting the app alone does not delete the account on the server. If you want your account and data deleted, please delete the account in the ways described in section 6.

  7. In the following cases data is kept for the stated period and is not used for any other purpose.

    a. Identification information and sanction records of accounts restricted for abuse: 1 year, to prevent abuse through re-registration

    b. When retention is required by applicable laws

    i. Records on contracts or withdrawal of offers: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce of Korea)

    ii. Records on payment and supply of goods: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce of Korea)

    iii. Records on consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce of Korea)

    iv. Access logs: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act of Korea)

  8. Personal data in electronic files is deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

6. Users’ Rights and How to Exercise Them

  1. In the game at ‘Lobby > Settings’, users can check their Player ID, account type (guest or Google), nickname and last server save time, change the nickname (once) and link a Google account.

  2. Users can withdraw consent and request deletion of their account and data at any time at ‘Lobby > Settings > Delete Account’, handled as described in section 5, item 2. Guest accounts can be requested the same way.

  3. If the app is already deleted or the user cannot access the game, the user can request deletion by sending the Player ID, nickname and other details to the email address in section 10 as explained on the account deletion page. There is no need to reinstall the app. The Company handles the request as described in section 5, item 2 after confirming the user’s identity, and never asks for a password. For guest accounts, processing may be limited if there is no information, such as the Player ID, that identifies the account, because identity confirmation is difficult.

  4. Users who wish to access, correct, delete or suspend the processing of personal data that cannot be checked in the game can also make requests to the email address in section 10 or through ‘Lobby > Settings > Contact Us’.

  5. Depending on the laws of the country where they live, users may request restriction of processing, object to processing, or request transfer of their data to another business, and may withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

  6. The Company informs the user of the result within 10 days of receiving a request. If the laws of the country where the user lives set a shorter period, the Company follows that period.

  7. When a user requests correction of an error in personal data, the Company does not use or provide that data until the correction is complete.

  8. If personal data essential to providing the Service is deleted, the Service cannot be used.

7. Personal Data of Children Under 14

The Service may be used only by persons aged 14 or older. The Company does not collect personal data of children under 14 and does not offer a way to use the Service with the consent of a legal guardian. Users confirm being 14 or older when first starting the game; without this confirmation no account is created and no data is sent to the server. The Company does not ask for a date of birth. If the Company learns that data of a child under 14 has been collected, it deletes the data without delay.

8. Automatic Data Collection Tools and How to Refuse Them

The Service is provided as an app and does not use cookies. However, advertising companies’ programs may collect the advertising identifier. Users can refuse this on their device as follows.

- Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)

9. Security Measures for Personal Data

  1. Personal data is kept on authenticated servers (BACKND), and communication between the device and the server is transmitted over encrypted connections.

  2. Access to the management console that handles personal data is kept to a minimum number of people, and those accounts are managed securely.

  3. The Company does not collect or keep passwords. Google account sign-in is done through Google’s authentication process.

  4. If the Company learns that personal data has been leaked, it informs users without delay and takes the necessary measures.
