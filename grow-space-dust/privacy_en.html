Creating an account does not require entering a name, phone number or address, and the Company does not collect passwords directly. The data above is processed through user input, use of the relevant feature, or automatic collection by SDKs.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Data is used only for account management and age confirmation, recording and processing consent and account deletion requests, saving and recovering game data, rankings, ads and ad rewards, payment verification, refunds and recovery, preventing duplicate rewards and abuse, handling customer inquiries, and statistics to improve the Service.

Where applicable law, such as in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, distinguishes legal bases for processing personal data, the Company processes data as follows.

  1. Account authentication and management, game data saving and recovery and rankings requested by the user, provision of purchased products and rewards, and responses to inquiries are processed to the extent necessary to perform the contract with the user.

  2. Statutory retention of contract, payment and dispute records and handling requests required by law are processed to comply with legal obligations applicable to the Company.

  3. Account and service security, prevention of abuse and duplicate rewards, essential diagnostics and limited usage statistics are processed to the extent necessary on the basis of legitimate interests permitted by law. These interests do not override users’ rights and freedoms.

Processing that requires consent by law relies on consent for the relevant purpose, which users may withdraw as described in section 7. Acceptance of the Terms is not treated as providing all consents required for advertising and analytics. Section 6 describes the restriction settings currently sent to advertising SDKs.

4. Retention and Destruction

Data is deleted without delay when its collection purpose has been achieved or the retention period below ends. Electronic files are deleted in a way that prevents recovery.

  1. When account deletion is requested, use of the account is restricted immediately. Data is kept for 30 days to allow cancellation of the request and then deleted. If immediate deletion is requested, data is deleted immediately after confirmation.

  2. Records of accounts sanctioned for abuse are kept for 1 year. Records required by applicable laws are kept for the required periods: contract and payment records for 5 years, dispute resolution records for 3 years, and access logs for 3 months.

  3. Nicknames and scores already shown in rankings may remain visible until that ranking period ends, for at most 1 month.

  4. Data stored on the device can be cleared with [Clear data on this device] in the game or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account on the server.
